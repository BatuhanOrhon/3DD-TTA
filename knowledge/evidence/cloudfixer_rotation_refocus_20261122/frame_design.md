Estimate a surface normal from the smallest eigenvector of the covariance of the 16 distinct nearest neighbors for each of up to 256 randomly chosen distinct valid coordinates, deterministic seed 20261122 per object. Weight by (middle − smallest eigenvalue) / largest eigenvalue. Score each proper rotation by the weighted mean of the squared largest absolute component of the rotated unit normals. Search identity and signed 10°, 20°, 30° rotations about the 13 unoriented axis, face-diagonal and body-diagonal directions of a cube. The bank is fixed for all inputs. Each half of the sampled patches estimates its own best rotation. Use the pooled best if the two winners differ by at most 10° and it gains more than 0.015 over identity on each half. Otherwise return identity. For a rotation of a known cloud, normal vectors transform by the same rotation. Report applied count, better/worse/tied angle count, and mean residual geodesic angle against the injected pose. The diagnostic cannot measure classification accuracy, Colab latency, or behavior on other corruptions. Do not optimize these constants on the results.
