[Paper] Both branches use the frozen XYZ prior of Shim, Kim and Yang's [CloudFixer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16193) and selective Chamfer observation fidelity adapted from Dastmalchi et al.'s [3DD-TTA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14495). The geometry arm uses [Kabsch](https://doi.org/10.1107/S0567739476001873) and repeated, trimmed correspondence estimation inspired by [Chetverikov et al., Trimmed ICP](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICPR.2002.1047997). For the determinant-corrected SVD, cite [Lawrence, Bernal and Witzgall, §2](https://doi.org/10.6028/jres.124.028). The search arm adapts the identity prior, confidence and angle-magnitude regularization from [Park and Park, CanDO, §§3.2–3.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.34850v1) to a fixed seven-pose search. Output-only PM and transport feedback are the integrations evaluated here. Welsch weighting remains an optional follow-up with the [Zhang, Yao and Deng](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.07627) citation if implemented.
